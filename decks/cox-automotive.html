02 / 07

What I see

  • Atlanta-based, roughly $10B automotive services and software company spanning Autotrader, KBB, Manheim, Dealertrack, Dealer.com, NextGear and more.
  • 29,000+ employees and 2.3 billion online interactions a year, per Cox's own CES 2026 piece.
  • Agentic development is moving fast: 100 of 680 scrum teams switched in March, with all teams targeted by end of 2026.
  • Fullpath closed in June 2026, adding an AI customer data platform aimed at 40,000+ dealer relationships.
03 / 07

The opportunity

  • From the outside, it looks like identity and data are split across a dozen brands. Fullpath needs one clean customer view, or the AI suite underperforms.
  • From the outside, it looks like agent evaluation is still being defined. Drift can mean hallucinations or deleted data, and that risk grows with every team.
  • From the outside, it looks like dealers lack data readiness. Cox says AI is a competitive divide, so customer-facing AI must work on messy dealer data.
04 / 07

What others are doing

  • OPENLANE launched OPENLANE Intelligence (Jan 2026), unifying AI tools like damage detection and engine-audio analysis inside condition reports.
  • CarGurus has added AI for search and dealership visits, focused on consumer discovery (date and details unconfirmed, verify).
  • Those are strong features inside single workflows. Cox could stand apart with unified data across the whole vehicle lifecycle, if it is clean.

My 30 / 60 / 90 day plan

  • 90 days: Launch the first identity resolution and stewardship model on a priority data set, with measurable gains and a roadmap for the other brands.
