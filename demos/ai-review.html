Casa Sol Taquería

Family-owned · 2 locations on Buford Highway · 14 staff · Prepared by Brittany May, A Little Agency

Your biggest opportunity

Phone orders and repeat questions are eating your evenings. About 40 calls a night ask the same five things: hours, the menu, catering, parking and whether you deliver. An AI assistant on your website and Google listing can answer those in English and Spanish, take catering inquiries, and free up the person stuck on the phone during the dinner rush.

Top 5 places AI fits

Quick wins this week

  • Turn on AI-suggested replies for Google reviews, and answer the last 30 in one sitting.
  • Paste your menu into an AI assistant and get a bilingual menu, a catering menu and allergen notes in an hour.
  • Set up a free scheduling tool so staff can trade shifts without group texts.

Bigger projects for later

  • A website chat and text assistant that answers questions and takes catering leads.
  • One customer list from your POS, catering emails and loyalty sign-ups, so you can actually reach regulars.
  • A simple weekly dashboard: sales, top dishes, food cost and labor in one place.

Risks to watch

  • Wrong answers. The assistant only answers from your real menu and hours, and hands anything else to a person.
  • Customer privacy. No card numbers or personal details go into AI tools. Payment stays in your POS.
  • Your voice. Every review reply and post is checked by you or a manager before it goes out.

Your 30-day starter plan

  1. Week 1Review replies, bilingual menu, shift scheduling.
  2. Week 2Clean up the customer list and pick one email or text tool.
  3. Week 3Launch the website and Google assistant for hours, menu and catering questions.
  4. Week 4Check what saved time, fix what didn't, and decide on the next project.

Tool costs are typical monthly prices and vary by plan. Hours saved are estimates from the owner's own numbers.
